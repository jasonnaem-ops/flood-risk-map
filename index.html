Raritan Corridor Flood Risk

A composite risk surface built from ground elevation and proximity to waterways, gridded across the New Brunswick–Piscataway reach of the Raritan River.

grid summary

Method. Each point's risk score blends normalized elevation (60%) and normalized distance to the nearest mapped waterway (40%) — lower ground and closer proximity both raise the score. Elevation from Open-Elevation; waterway geometry from OpenStreetMap.
This is a simplified overlay model for portfolio and educational purposes — not a certified flood study. It does not incorporate hydrology, storm-surge modeling, or FEMA flood zone data. The river line is the actual mapped Raritan River channel; the base grid marks latitude/longitude, not roads or parcels.
